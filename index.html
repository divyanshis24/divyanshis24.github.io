National finalist · top 35 of 30,000+ Goldman Sachs India Hackathon · Jun 2026

Quant engine: ETF replication, swaption pricing & systematic strategy

Built an ETF proxy engine replicating 5 illiquid bond ETFs from 22 liquid ETFs, futures and CDX via Ridge factor models, with NAV, DV01/CS01 and cost-weighted Lasso hedging. Priced Bermudan swaptions end to end — yield curve construction, Jamshidian-based Hull–White calibration and a trinomial tree. Designed an adaptive 100-asset weekly strategy with multi-factor signals, inverse-volatility sizing and a regime overlay, cutting drawdown from 10.8% to 7.7% and turnover from 31 to 12.

Course project, ECO723 · Nov 2025

Payment Fraud Risk Analytics

A LightGBM fraud scorer over 590K card transactions with 150 engineered features from UID behavioural aggregates, rolling-window entity history and PCA-compressed V blocks. Time-gapped forward CV plus adversarial validation to prune drift-heavy features scored ROC-AUC 0.953 and PR-AUC 0.702 on a 94.6K forward-in-time holdout — a $3.27M annualised loss reduction at under 1% false declines.

  • Feature engineering
  • Calibration
  • SQL

Agentic RAG Chatbot

SURGE research · May – Jul 2024

ARIMA–LSTM Hybrid for Nifty 50 Forecasting

Undergraduate research under Prof. Sanjiv Kumar at IIT Kanpur. Built an ARIMA–LSTM hybrid for Nifty 50 return forecasting, using a Random Forest on squared residuals for lag selection. Modelled mean structure via ARIMA(2,1,2) and volatility clustering via GARCH(5,2), validated with ADF and Ljung–Box tests, then benchmarked all three models on RMSE, MAPE and MASE.
